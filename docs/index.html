glucose-pipeline-lab

Ingest glucose sensor readings, reject and count the bad ones, store them so a retry is safe, compute the standard CGM report metrics, and serve them over a REST API. The insight layer can say what the numbers show against the consensus targets, and nothing else.

Source on GitHub

Synthetic data only. Not a medical device, no FDA, ISO or HIPAA claim, and the metrics were not validated against clinical data.

What is in it

Safe to retry

Ingest is idempotent on subject and timestamp. A client that times out can resend the whole batch. Out-of-order rows are accepted.

Bad data is visible

Out-of-range, future, missing and zero timestamps are rejected with a reason, returned to the sender and counted by reason.

Grounded insights

An insight is kept only if its evidence matches the summary field and its text has no dosing or treatment words. A future model plugs in behind the same guard.

Data lifecycle

The log replays on start, and a purge drops readings older than a cutoff and rewrites the log.

Observable

Prometheus counters for accepted, duplicate and rejected rows by reason, and request count and time per route.

Tested over a socket

13 tests, including ingest to summary over a real HTTP server, token checks and an oversized batch. CI also boots the jar and queries it.

Not covered
